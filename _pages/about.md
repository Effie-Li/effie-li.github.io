I am a Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. I am broadly interested in machine and human intelligence, with a recent focus on human-AI communication, alignment, AI safety, and human growth. Some of my recent public work explores [visual communication](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36449) (including [visual prompt engineering](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.25537)), [fuzzy/miscommunication](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23939), and [subtle communication](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05273) between humans and AI systems.

I also work on mechanistic interpretability ([in-context representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04466) and [structured computation](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=oFC2LAqS6Z) in transformers) and [human cognition](https://www.proquest.com/openview/763a960127f6ed96cfc444de1b7cba61/1?pq-origsite=gscholar&cbl=18750&diss=y). I also spent time on [social intelligence benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22152) and [embodied AI](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=oxvgIVWGo1) as interns at Meta and AI2, and built [neural decoders](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46926-0) of human episodic memory at UPenn.
